Blind source-grounding checklist (before viewing native labels)
1. Inspect final actual model request, not only result archive. Each cited ID and literal quote must appear in that request. Record missing/ambiguous citations.
2. Distinguish observed configuration, observed failure and inferred causal relation. Check explanation against actually consumed configuration/logs. Do not infer unique cause from absence of a record or promote an unmeasured causal claim to a measurement.
3. Distinguish consistent-but-old evidence at historical execution from evidence valid after the notification. For each consumed stamped source, inspect authenticity and freshness against the branch's current revision heads/archive. Record stale input count and any stale facts in answer. Root label correctness alone does not repair outdated evidence.
4. Uncertain with a sound explanation is appropriate abstention but not successful diagnosis. Invalid or unsupported diagnosed answers remain unsuccessful regardless of native root score.
5. Linked corrections retain historical publication and cannot overwrite newer state. Local publication testing is not evidence of actual Slack/service remediation.

Native and joint outcome
Run native scoring after inference; report JRA, ECR and contract/budget checks separately. Benchmark process-path requirements may differ from semantic sufficiency. Preserve both, with no post-hoc score replacement. Primary joint success requires diagnosed + JRA1 + ECR1 + valid contract + current supporting evidence + no stale publication accepted. Semantic review is recorded judgment, not a validated universal checker.
No-recovery arm retains the shared historical diagnosis; score only its POST-notification adequacy. Do not claim the historical answer was incorrect before the constructed update.

Accounting and timing
Keep no-recovery, restart, selective and fallback outcomes all in denominator, including skipped/not-injected/failed. Prefix charged once physically and to each arm logically. Native verifier arm starts independently and is not part of shared-prefix recovery savings. Report full end-to-end and incremental amounts; failures consume resources too. Unknown usage uses declared reservation, not invented actual usage. CPU timing only for synchronous measured sections; whole async wall time includes model wait. This pilot does not claim complete per-event tracking CPU instrumentation or dollar costs.
Do not compare a failed/uncertain arm's completion time as quality-qualified latency. Costs conditional on two successful arms are secondary. Single order and one case cannot establish significance. Resource/source measurements and review costs not captured in the task model ledger are stated as limitations.

No automatic retries. If the fixed change was not exposed, report not_injected and stop related comparisons. The next step is protocol review on development, not selecting a case to force a favorable outcome.
