C. Evidence interpretation and limitations
Parallel-one explicitly lacks cartservice environment configuration and refuses a confident diagnosis. Telemetry-first-one cites REDIS_ADDR=redis-cart-invalid:6379, the redis-cart service on the same port, and observed connection/timeouts. Parallel-two cites the configured bad hostname, failed connections to it, and successful connectivity to redis-cart. A separate source-grounding review is recorded below when completed; native scores alone do not prove semantic truth.
Telemetry-first-one uses 17893 fewer tokens (21.99%) than parallel-two in this run, but those arms have different round limits and different ECR outcomes: this is NOT a matched quality-preserving savings estimate. The equal-round topology comparison is parallel-one versus telemetry-first-one; increased collection dependence obtains evidence here at additional initial cost/serial latency.
The controlled matrix separately shows that this dependence raises telemetry-expiry recovery from one to two model steps. Combining these findings motivates a joint study; it does not measure real recovery tokens or a break-even threshold. Do not add simulated units to real tokens.
Single known case, fixed order, mutable model alias and one new repetition: no population accuracy, latency significance, novel-family generalization or production readiness claim. Historical same-case outcomes remain development observations. No new semantic recovery/verification/missing-provenance model experiment occurred in this batch.

D. Decision
The plan is executable and produces discriminating outcomes, including negative controls. Proceed with P2: explicit missing-provenance fallback, unified source-consistency baseline, incorrect/stale-content controls, tracking overhead and topology comparisons under equal round ceilings. Keep scheduling as first optional extension and learning second. Do not launch the held-out ceiling until whole-batch budget and scoring are frozen. Existing Phase1 archive unchanged; current supplementary report needs reconciliation before course submission.
