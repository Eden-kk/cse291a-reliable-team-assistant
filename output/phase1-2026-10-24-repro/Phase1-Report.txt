Costs, limits and next steps

What the experiment establishes

Selective recovery used 1 new call versus 3 for restart in each of 4 eligible units: 33,260 versus 64,902 aggregate incremental tokens, 48.8% fewer. This excludes shared-prefix costs and is not end-to-end service savings. Equal 3/4 quality pass counts do not prove per-case equivalence or statistical non-inferiority. When all work expired, both methods needed 3 calls on all 6 units, demonstrating the conservative restart boundary.

No corrupted port was adopted in the four corrupted answers. This batch therefore supports invalidation/recomputation behavior and cost bounds, not correction of a propagated wrong diagnosis. Two service units had no eligible returned YAML field; no retargeting or rerun was performed. Service answers lacked discriminating evidence; other failures included wrong root labels, malformed quotations and insufficient distinct citations. Completion is not quality success.
