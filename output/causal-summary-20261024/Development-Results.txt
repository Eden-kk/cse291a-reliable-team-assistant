Limitations and decision
The brief prompt explicitly warns that summaries are fallible and exposes raw evidence. This favors prevention, which is a legitimate result but restricts the question tested. A fixed extra adservice YAML read and a known seen-case intervention make eligibility deliberate; this is not natural error prevalence. All arms use a new summary step absent from the frozen formal workflow. One case, one run, fixed restart-before-selective order, no independent human review. Do not rerun until a favorable propagation outcome appears.
Next protocol should distinguish (a) raw-visible coordinator and (b) a realistic, bandwidth-limited specialist handoff with source references and on-demand raw retrieval. Specify this information boundary for all arms before inference, not only for the faulty arm; allow retrieval/abstention as prevention outcomes. Compare accepted false conclusions, affected downstream actions, correction quality and quality-qualified cost. Use new development cases before selecting a genuinely unseen confirmatory set. If realistic information boundaries still prevent propagation, report recovery primarily as validity/expiry repair and quantify the strong prevention baseline.
